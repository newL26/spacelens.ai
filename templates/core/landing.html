Workflow

How SpaceLens AI Works

From raw satellite image to actionable intelligence in seconds.

01

Upload Image

Drag & drop a satellite or aerial image (JPG, PNG, GeoTIFF).

02

Preprocess

Image is resized, normalised and prepared for model inference.

03

Classify

TensorFlow/Keras model outputs probability scores across 10 land-cover classes.

04

Granite Prompt

Top-3 probabilities are sent to IBM Granite with an intelligence-report prompt.

05

Report

Full AI report is saved to PostgreSQL and displayed with a probability chart.

06

Download PDF

One-click export of the complete intelligence report as a PDF document.
